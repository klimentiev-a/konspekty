## Theory

## Cash is a fact, profit is an opinion

There is an old saying in finance: "Profit is an opinion, cash is a fact" (прибыль — это мнение, деньги — это факт). Profit depends on accounting choices: how fast you depreciate a machine, when you recognise revenue, what you call a one-off. Cash in the bank account does not depend on opinions. That is why investors, lenders and valuation models (модели оценки) focus on cash flow (денежный поток).

The cash flow statement (отчёт о движении денежных средств) explains why the cash balance changed during the period. It has three sections.

## Section 1: cash flow from operations (CFO)

Cash flow from operating activities (денежный поток от операционной деятельности) is the cash generated by the core business. Under the indirect method (косвенный метод), which almost all companies use, it starts from net income and makes two kinds of adjustments:

1. **Add back non-cash expenses (неденежные расходы).** Depreciation and amortization reduced profit, but no cash left the company. The same for impairments (обесценение) and share-based compensation (вознаграждение акциями).
2. **Adjust for changes in working capital (изменение оборотного капитала).** If receivables grew by 50, customers owe 50 more than before, so cash is 50 lower than profit: subtract 50. If payables grew, the company delayed paying suppliers, so cash is higher: add. If inventory grew, cash was spent on stock: subtract.

> **CFO = Net income + D&A + other non-cash items − Increase in working capital.**

A healthy company has CFO higher than net income, because D&A is added back. If CFO is consistently lower than net income, profit may be of low quality (низкое качество прибыли): sales are booked but not collected.

## Section 2: cash flow from investing (CFI)

Cash flow from investing activities (от инвестиционной деятельности) is usually negative. It includes capital expenditures, capex (капитальные затраты) on new equipment and buildings, purchases of other companies (acquisitions, поглощения), and purchases of financial assets. Sales of assets (divestments, продажа активов) are inflows.

Analysts split capex into maintenance capex (поддерживающие капзатраты), needed just to keep the business running, and growth capex (капзатраты на рост), spent to expand. Companies rarely disclose the split, so it is often estimated as roughly equal to depreciation.

## Section 3: cash flow from financing (CFF)

Cash flow from financing activities (от финансовой деятельности) shows money moving between the company and its investors: debt raised (привлечение долга) is an inflow, debt repaid (погашение долга) an outflow; shares issued (выпуск акций) an inflow; dividends paid (выплата дивидендов) and share buybacks (обратный выкуп акций) are outflows.

The sum of the three sections, plus the effect of exchange rates (курсовые разницы) on foreign-currency cash, equals the change in cash for the period.

## Free cash flow: what is really left

Free cash flow, FCF (свободный денежный поток) is the cash the company generates after the investment needed to keep and grow the business. The simplest definition:

> **FCF = CFO − Capex.**

This is the cash available to repay debt, pay dividends, buy back shares or make acquisitions. FCF yield (доходность по свободному денежному потоку) = FCF / Market capitalization. In Russia, dividend policies (дивидендная политика) of companies such as Lukoil are tied to FCF, so investors watch it closely.

## FCFF and FCFE: two versions for valuation

For valuation (Topic 8) we need two more precise versions.

**Free cash flow to the firm, FCFF (свободный денежный поток для фирмы)** is the cash available to all capital providers (поставщики капитала), both lenders and shareholders, before any interest payments:

> **FCFF = EBIT × (1 − tax rate) + D&A − Capex − Increase in NWC.**

EBIT × (1 − t) is called NOPAT (Net Operating Profit After Tax, чистая операционная прибыль после налогов). We start from EBIT, not net income, because interest has not been subtracted yet: FCFF is "unlevered" (без учёта долга). Discounting FCFF at WACC gives enterprise value (стоимость предприятия).

**Free cash flow to equity, FCFE (свободный денежный поток для акционеров)** is what is left for shareholders only, after interest and after net borrowing:

> **FCFE = FCFF − Interest × (1 − tax rate) + Net borrowing.**

Net borrowing (чистое привлечение долга) is new debt minus repayments. FCFE is "levered" (с учётом долга). Discounting FCFE at the cost of equity gives equity value (стоимость собственного капитала).

## Why cash and profit diverge: a checklist

| Situation | Effect on profit | Effect on cash |
|---|---|---|
| Big investment in new plant | Small (depreciation starts) | Large outflow now |
| Customers pay 90 days late | None | Lower CFO |
| Inventory build-up before season | None | Lower CFO |
| Goodwill impairment | Large loss | None |
| Dividend paid | None | Outflow in CFF |
| Debt refinanced | None | Inflow and outflow in CFF |

## Cash burn and runway

For start-ups (стартапы) and loss-making companies the vocabulary is different. Cash burn (сжигание денег) is how much cash the company loses per month. Runway (запас хода) is how many months of cash it has left: cash divided by monthly burn. "We have 18 months of runway" means the company must raise money or become profitable within 18 months.

> **Remember.** CFO starts with net income, adds back non-cash expenses, subtracts working capital growth. FCF = CFO − Capex. FCFF is for everyone (discount at WACC), FCFE is for shareholders (discount at cost of equity).
