## Theory

## Why ratios

A financial ratio (финансовый коэффициент) connects two numbers from the statements so that companies of different sizes and years can be compared. Ratio analysis (коэффициентный анализ) answers four questions: Is the company profitable? Can it pay its bills? How much debt does it carry? How efficiently does it use its assets? A ratio is only meaningful against a benchmark (ориентир, база для сравнения): the same company last year, or peers (аналоги) this year.

## Profitability ratios

Profitability ratios (коэффициенты рентабельности) show how much profit the company makes from its sales, assets or equity.

- **Gross margin, EBITDA margin, net margin** (Topic 3): profit lines divided by revenue.
- **Return on equity, ROE (рентабельность собственного капитала)** = Net income / Average equity. How much the owners earn on their capital. Banks are judged mainly on ROE; Sberbank's 22–25% is considered strong.
- **Return on assets, ROA (рентабельность активов)** = Net income / Average assets.
- **Return on invested capital, ROIC (рентабельность инвестированного капитала)** = NOPAT / (Debt + Equity). The cleanest measure: it compares operating profit after tax with all capital used. If ROIC is above WACC, the company creates value (создаёт стоимость); if below, it destroys value even while profitable. The difference ROIC − WACC is called the spread (спред), and the money amount is economic value added, EVA (экономическая добавленная стоимость).

## The DuPont formula

The DuPont analysis (формула Дюпона) breaks ROE into three drivers:

> **ROE = Net margin × Asset turnover × Equity multiplier**
> = (Net income / Revenue) × (Revenue / Assets) × (Assets / Equity).

A supermarket has a low margin but high turnover (оборачиваемость); a luxury brand has a high margin and low turnover; a bank has thin margins and a huge equity multiplier (мультипликатор капитала), that is, high leverage. The formula shows whether ROE comes from efficiency or just from debt. Rising ROE driven only by the equity multiplier is a warning.

## Liquidity ratios

Liquidity ratios (коэффициенты ликвидности) show whether the company can pay short-term obligations (краткосрочные обязательства).

- **Current ratio (коэффициент текущей ликвидности)** = Current assets / Current liabilities. Around 1.5–2 is comfortable; below 1 means current liabilities exceed current assets.
- **Quick ratio, acid test (коэффициент быстрой ликвидности)** = (Current assets − Inventory) / Current liabilities. Stricter, because inventory may be hard to sell quickly.
- **Cash ratio (коэффициент абсолютной ликвидности)** = Cash / Current liabilities.

## Leverage and coverage ratios

Leverage ratios (коэффициенты долговой нагрузки) measure how much debt the company uses.

- **Net debt / EBITDA (чистый долг к EBITDA):** how many years of EBITDA it would take to repay net debt. Below 2x is comfortable for most industries; above 4x is highly leveraged (с высокой долговой нагрузкой).
- **Debt / Equity, D/E (долг к собственному капиталу):** the capital structure. Also expressed as debt-to-capital, D/(D+E).
- **Interest coverage ratio, ICR (коэффициент покрытия процентов)** = EBIT / Interest expense. How many times operating profit covers interest. Below 1.5x the company is in trouble; rating agencies (рейтинговые агентства) watch it closely.
- **Debt service coverage ratio, DSCR (коэффициент покрытия обслуживания долга)** = Cash available for debt service / (Interest + Principal). Used in project finance (проектное финансирование) and by banks.

## Efficiency ratios

Efficiency ratios (коэффициенты оборачиваемости, деловой активности) show how well the company uses its assets.

- **Asset turnover (оборачиваемость активов)** = Revenue / Average assets.
- **Inventory turnover (оборачиваемость запасов)** = COGS / Average inventory. In days: DIO (Topic 4).
- **Receivables turnover (оборачиваемость дебиторской задолженности)** = Revenue / Average receivables. In days: DSO.
- **Payables days, DPO** and the **cash conversion cycle** complete the picture.

## Market ratios

Market ratios (рыночные коэффициенты) link the share price with fundamentals: P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA (Topic 9), dividend yield (дивидендная доходность) and payout ratio (коэффициент выплат) = Dividends / Net income. A payout ratio above 100% means the company pays more than it earns, which cannot last unless FCF is higher than profit.

## Reading ratios like an analyst

1. **Trend (динамика):** three to five years. Direction matters more than one number.
2. **Peers:** compare with the industry median.
3. **Consistency:** if margins rise but cash flow falls, dig deeper.
4. **Context:** a utility can carry 4x Net debt/EBITDA because its cash flows are stable; a miner cannot.
5. **Red flags (тревожные сигналы):** receivables growing faster than revenue, inventory piling up, interest coverage falling, frequent "adjusted" numbers, auditor changes.

## A short example

Company X: revenue 1,000, net income 80, assets 800, equity 400, net debt 300, EBITDA 200, interest 40.

- Net margin 8%, asset turnover 1.25, equity multiplier 2.0 → ROE = 8% × 1.25 × 2 = 20%.
- Net debt / EBITDA = 1.5x, interest coverage = EBIT / 40; if EBIT is 150, ICR = 3.75x.
- Conclusion: profitable, moderately leveraged, comfortable coverage.

> **Remember.** Four families: profitability, liquidity, leverage, efficiency. ROE = margin × turnover × leverage. ROIC above WACC means value is created. A ratio without a benchmark is just a number.
