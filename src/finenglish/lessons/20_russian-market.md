## Theory

## The infrastructure

The Russian equity market (российский рынок акций) is concentrated on one venue: the Moscow Exchange, MOEX (Московская биржа), formed in 2011 from the merger of MICEX and RTS. The group owns the exchange, the clearing house NCC (Национальный клиринговый центр, НКЦ) and the central depository NSD (Национальный расчётный депозитарий, НРД). The St Petersburg Exchange, SPB (СПБ Биржа) used to trade foreign shares; after sanctions in 2023 that business largely stopped.

The regulator (регулятор) is the Bank of Russia (Банк России, ЦБ РФ), which is both the central bank and the supervisor of banks, brokers, insurers and the stock market: a mega-regulator (мегарегулятор). Trading is in roubles, settlement T+1, main session 10:00–18:50 Moscow time, plus morning and evening sessions.

## The indices

The MOEX Russia Index, IMOEX (индекс Мосбиржи) is the rouble benchmark of about 40–50 largest and most liquid stocks, weighted by free-float market cap with a cap on single names. The RTS Index (индекс РТС) is the same basket in dollars, so it falls when the rouble weakens. Sector indices (отраслевые индексы) cover oil and gas, financials, metals and mining, IT, consumer, electricity, transport. The RGBI (индекс государственных облигаций) tracks OFZ prices; the RUCBI family tracks corporate bonds.

## The main issuers

Russian blue chips (голубые фишки) are dominated by commodities and state-linked companies.

| Sector | Companies | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| Oil and gas | Lukoil, Rosneft, Gazprom, Novatek, Surgutneftegas, Tatneft, Gazprom Neft | Largest weight; earnings follow oil prices and the rouble; Surgutneftegas preferred shares pay dividends from FX gains |
| Banks | Sberbank, VTB, T-Technologies (T-Bank), Sovcombank, Bank Saint Petersburg | Sber is the most traded stock and the biggest dividend payer |
| Metals and mining | Norilsk Nickel, Polyus, Severstal, NLMK, MMK, Rusal, Alrosa | Exporters, cyclical, sensitive to sanctions and China |
| Technology and internet | Yandex, Ozon, VK, HeadHunter, Positive Technologies, Astra | Growth names; many re-domiciled (переехали в российскую юрисдикцию) in 2023–2024 |
| Consumer | X5 Group, Magnit, Lenta, Fix Price | Domestic demand, inflation beneficiaries |
| Telecom, utilities, transport | MTS, Rostelecom, Inter RAO, Aeroflot, Transneft | Stable cash flows, regulated tariffs |

State-owned enterprises, SOEs (государственные компании) such as Gazprom, Rosneft, Sberbank and VTB make up a large share of the index; the government requires them to pay 50% of IFRS net income as dividends, which is a key driver of the market's dividend culture.

## Dividends: the main story

Russian stocks are among the highest-yielding in the world: dividend yields (дивидендная доходность) of 8–15% are common. Companies publish a dividend policy (дивидендная политика) linking payouts to net income or free cash flow; the board recommends (совет директоров рекомендует), the general meeting approves (собрание акционеров утверждает), and the stock drops by the dividend on the ex-dividend date (экс-дивидендная дата): the dividend gap (дивидендный гэп). Retail investors (частные инвесторы), who became the majority of trading volume after 2022, watch the dividend calendar closely.

## Bonds and the key rate

The government bond market is OFZ (ОФЗ, federal loan bonds), issued by the Ministry of Finance (Минфин) at weekly auctions (аукционы). Types: OFZ-PD with fixed coupon (с постоянным доходом), OFZ-PK floaters (с переменным купоном) tied to RUONIA, OFZ-IN inflation-linked (с индексируемым номиналом). Corporate bonds (корпоративные облигации) are mostly rouble-denominated; after 2022 replacement bonds (замещающие облигации) took the place of Eurobonds, and yuan bonds (юаневые облигации) appeared for exporters.

The key rate (ключевая ставка) of the Bank of Russia drives everything: it rose from 7.5% in mid-2023 to 21% in late 2024, the highest in two decades, then came down in 2025–2026 as inflation slowed. Floaters, money-market funds (фонды денежного рынка) and deposits (вклады) became the favourite instruments of retail investors during the high-rate period, drawing money away from stocks.

## Currency and capital controls

The rouble (рубль) floats, but with capital controls (ограничения на движение капитала) since 2022: limits on foreign-currency transfers abroad, mandatory sale of export revenue (обязательная продажа валютной выручки) at times, and a yuan-centred FX market (валютный рынок) because dollar and euro trading on MOEX stopped in June 2024 after US sanctions on the exchange. The official rate (официальный курс) is now set by the Bank of Russia from OTC data. The yuan (юань) is the main foreign currency traded; the dollar-rouble rate still drives sentiment.

## Sanctions and the post-2022 landscape

After February 2022 the market changed structurally. Foreign investors (иностранные инвесторы) from unfriendly countries (недружественные страны) had their holdings frozen in type-C accounts (счета типа «С»); their shares, about half of the free float, no longer trade. Depositary receipts (депозитарные расписки) on Russian companies were delisted from London and New York, and many companies converted them into local shares or re-domiciled (перерегистрировались) from Cyprus, the Netherlands and other jurisdictions to Russia or special administrative regions (специальные административные районы). Western banks and the Big Four left; local brokers and rating agencies grew. Disclosure (раскрытие информации) was partly suspended, and some companies still publish limited data, which makes analysis harder. Secondary sanctions (вторичные санкции) affect payments with China, Turkey and other partners.

The result: a market owned mostly by domestic retail investors, pension funds and local institutions, trading at low multiples (P/E around 4–6) with high dividend yields, highly sensitive to geopolitics (геополитика), oil and the key rate.

## Useful phrases for interviews

- "The Russian market is a dividend and commodity story: low valuations, high yields, high sensitivity to oil and the key rate."
- "Sber is the proxy for the whole market: the most liquid stock with the largest weight in the index."
- "OFZ yields reflect the Bank of Russia's policy; the curve was inverted while the key rate stayed above 15%."
- "After 2022 the investor base shifted to domestic retail; foreign holdings are frozen in type-C accounts."
- "The rouble is driven by the trade balance and capital controls rather than by interest rate differentials."

> **Remember.** One exchange, one regulator, one benchmark (IMOEX). Commodity-heavy and state-heavy index, huge dividend culture, OFZ for the risk-free rate, key rate as the main driver. Post-2022: domestic retail, frozen foreigners, yuan instead of dollar.
