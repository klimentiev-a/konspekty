## Theory

## Mergers, acquisitions and why they happen

Mergers and acquisitions, M&A (слияния и поглощения) is the business of combining companies. In a merger (слияние) two companies join to form one, often as equals. In an acquisition (поглощение, покупка) one company, the acquirer or buyer (покупатель), buys another, the target (целевая компания, объект сделки). In practice almost every deal is an acquisition; "merger of equals" (слияние равных) is usually a polite word.

Why buy a company instead of growing yourself? The main reasons:

- **Synergies (синергии):** the combined company earns more or spends less than the two separately. Cost synergies (синергии по затратам): one head office, bigger purchasing discounts. Revenue synergies (синергии по выручке): selling each other's products. Cost synergies are real more often than revenue synergies.
- **Market share (доля рынка)** and pricing power.
- **Speed:** buying a technology or a licence is faster than building it.
- **Vertical integration (вертикальная интеграция):** buying a supplier or a distributor. Horizontal integration (горизонтальная интеграция): buying a competitor. A conglomerate (конгломерат) buys unrelated businesses.
- **Undervaluation:** the target is cheap.

A strategic buyer (стратегический покупатель) is a company in the same or related industry, which can pay for synergies. A financial buyer (финансовый покупатель), such as a private equity fund, buys for returns alone (Topic 17).

## The deal process

A typical sell-side process (процесс продажи) run by an investment bank:

1. **Preparation.** The bank writes a teaser (тизер), a short anonymous description, and a confidential information memorandum, CIM (информационный меморандум), a long document with financials. Potential buyers sign a non-disclosure agreement, NDA (соглашение о неразглашении).
2. **First round.** Buyers submit non-binding offers (необязывающие предложения) with an indicative price range.
3. **Due diligence (комплексная проверка, дью-дилидженс).** Selected buyers get access to a data room (комната данных) and check financial, legal, tax and operational details with their advisers (консультанты): lawyers, auditors, consultants.
4. **Binding offers (обязывающие предложения)** and negotiation of the share purchase agreement, SPA (договор купли-продажи акций).
5. **Signing (подписание)** of the SPA. Conditions precedent (отлагательные условия) must then be met: antitrust approval (одобрение антимонопольного органа; в России ФАС), shareholder votes, regulatory consents.
6. **Closing (закрытие сделки):** money changes hands, ownership transfers. Between signing and closing can be months.

Letter of intent, LOI (письмо о намерениях) and term sheet (основные условия сделки) are the documents that fix the main terms before lawyers write the full agreement.

## Price and premium

The acquirer usually pays more than the market price: the control premium (премия за контроль), typically 20–40% over the undisturbed price (цена до появления слухов о сделке). The premium is justified by synergies: if the buyer pays a premium of 100 and expects synergies worth 150 in present value, the deal creates value for its shareholders; if synergies turn out to be 50, value was transferred to the target's shareholders. Studies show that most of the gains in M&A go to the sellers.

The price can be paid in cash (денежная сделка), in the acquirer's shares (сделка с оплатой акциями), or a mix. In a share deal the exchange ratio (коэффициент обмена) says how many acquirer shares each target share gets. An earn-out (отложенный платёж по результатам) pays part of the price later if the target hits targets, which helps when buyer and seller disagree about the future.

## Accretion and dilution

The question every board asks: will the deal increase or decrease our earnings per share, EPS? If EPS goes up, the deal is accretive (увеличивает прибыль на акцию); if down, dilutive (размывает прибыль на акцию). The quick test for an all-stock deal: if the acquirer's P/E is higher than the target's, the deal is accretive, because the buyer pays with "expensive" shares for "cheap" earnings. For a cash deal, compare the target's earnings yield with the after-tax cost of the debt used to pay: if the target yields more than the debt costs, accretive. Accretion is not the same as value creation, but markets watch it.

## Goodwill and purchase price allocation

When the buyer pays more than the fair value (справедливая стоимость) of the target's net assets, the difference is recorded as goodwill (гудвил) on the buyer's balance sheet. Purchase price allocation, PPA (распределение цены покупки) assigns the price to identifiable assets first, such as brands and customer lists, and the rest to goodwill. Goodwill is not amortised under IFRS but tested for impairment (обесценение) every year; a big write-off means the company admits it overpaid.

## Hostile takeovers and defences

Most deals are friendly (дружественные): the target's board agrees. In a hostile takeover (враждебное поглощение) the acquirer goes directly to shareholders with a tender offer (публичная оферта о выкупе акций) or tries to replace the board through a proxy fight (борьба за голоса акционеров). Defences include the poison pill (ядовитая пилюля): a plan that lets other shareholders buy shares cheaply if anyone crosses a threshold, diluting the raider; the white knight (белый рыцарь): a friendlier buyer invited by the target; staggered boards (ступенчатый совет директоров), golden parachutes (золотые парашюты) for managers, and simply buying back shares. In Russia a buyer who crosses 30%, 50% or 75% of a public company must make a mandatory offer (обязательное предложение) to all other shareholders at the same price.

## Divestitures and spin-offs

The opposite of acquisition: a divestiture (продажа подразделения) sells a business unit; a spin-off (выделение) gives shareholders shares in a new independent company; a carve-out (частичное выделение с IPO) sells part of a subsidiary to the public. These deals are common when a conglomerate discount (дисконт конгломерата) makes the parts worth more than the whole.

> **Remember.** M&A = buying growth and synergies. Process: teaser → NDA → due diligence → SPA → signing → closing. Premium is paid for control and synergies. Accretive if EPS rises. Overpaying shows up later as goodwill impairment.
