## Theory

## What a bond is

A bond (облигация) is a loan cut into pieces and sold to investors. The issuer (эмитент), a government or a company, borrows money and promises two things: to pay interest, called the coupon (купон), on fixed dates, and to repay the face value, also called par or principal (номинал, основная сумма долга), at maturity (погашение, дата погашения).

Example: a 1,000-rouble bond with a 12% annual coupon and three years to maturity pays 120 each year and 1,000 at the end. The coupon rate (купонная ставка) is fixed at issue; what changes is the price.

Government bonds (государственные облигации) in Russia are called OFZ (ОФЗ, облигации федерального займа); in the US, Treasuries (казначейские облигации); in the UK, gilts. Corporate bonds (корпоративные облигации) are issued by companies. Bonds with maturity under a year are bills (векселя, краткосрочные бумаги); long ones, up to 30 years, are often called long bonds.

## Price and yield move in opposite directions

This is the most important rule of the bond market. If market interest rates (рыночные ставки) rise, new bonds pay higher coupons, so old bonds with lower coupons become less attractive and their prices fall. When rates fall, old bond prices rise.

Bond prices are quoted as a percentage of par: 95 means 950 roubles for a 1,000 bond. A bond below 100 trades at a discount (с дисконтом); above 100, at a premium (с премией).

The yield to maturity, YTM (доходность к погашению) is the total return you get if you buy the bond today and hold it to maturity, with all coupons reinvested. It is the IRR of the bond's cash flows (Topic 6). Current yield (текущая доходность) is simpler: coupon / price. If a 12% coupon bond trades at 95, current yield is 12.6% and YTM is higher still, because you also gain 5 points when it is repaid at par.

## Duration: sensitivity to rates

Duration (дюрация) measures how much a bond's price changes when yields change. Modified duration (модифицированная дюрация) of 5 means the price falls about 5% if yields rise by 1 percentage point. Longer maturity and lower coupon mean higher duration. Long OFZ with 15 years to maturity have a duration around 7–8, so they are very sensitive to the key rate (ключевая ставка); short OFZ barely move. Investors who expect rates to fall buy long duration; those who fear rate rises stay short.

## Credit risk, ratings and spreads

A government that prints its own currency almost never defaults (объявляет дефолт) on local-currency bonds, so its yield is the risk-free rate (безрисковая ставка). A company might default, so investors demand extra yield: the credit spread (кредитный спред), measured in basis points, bps (базисные пункты; 1 bp = 0.01%). "The bond trades at 300 bps over OFZ" means its yield is 3 percentage points above the government curve.

Credit rating agencies (рейтинговые агентства) grade issuers. Globally: S&P, Moody's, Fitch; in Russia: ACRA (АКРА) and Expert RA (Эксперт РА). Ratings from AAA down to BBB− are investment grade (инвестиционный уровень); BB+ and below are high yield or junk (высокодоходные, «мусорные»). A downgrade (понижение рейтинга) widens the spread and lowers the price.

Other risks: liquidity risk (риск ликвидности), the inability to sell quickly; reinvestment risk (риск реинвестирования), coupons reinvested at lower rates; inflation risk (инфляционный риск), fixed coupons losing purchasing power.

## Types of bonds

| Type | Feature | Russian |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed-rate bond | Coupon fixed for life | Облигация с постоянным купоном |
| Floating-rate note, floater | Coupon tied to a reference rate, for example RUONIA or key rate + spread | Флоатер, облигация с плавающим купоном |
| Inflation-linked bond | Principal grows with inflation (OFZ-IN, US TIPS) | Облигация с индексируемым номиналом, линкер |
| Zero-coupon bond | No coupon, sold at a deep discount | Бескупонная облигация |
| Callable bond | Issuer may repay early | Облигация с правом досрочного выкупа эмитентом (колл-опцион) |
| Puttable bond | Investor may demand early repayment, in Russia via an offer (оферта) | Облигация с офертой (пут-опцион) |
| Convertible bond | Can be exchanged for shares | Конвертируемая облигация |
| Eurobond | Issued outside the home country, usually in dollars or euros | Еврооблигация |

In 2024–2025 floaters became very popular in Russia because the key rate was high and uncertain: their coupons rise with the rate, so their prices stay near par.

## The yield curve

The yield curve (кривая доходности) plots yields of government bonds against maturity. A normal curve (нормальная кривая) slopes upward: long bonds yield more, because investors want compensation for time and uncertainty. An inverted curve (инвертированная кривая), where short yields are above long ones, usually appears when the central bank has raised rates sharply and the market expects cuts later; it is a classic recession (рецессия) signal in the US. The Russian curve was inverted through much of 2024–2025 for exactly that reason: short OFZ yielded more than long ones.

Bond indices: the RGBI (индекс государственных облигаций Мосбиржи) tracks OFZ prices. When RGBI rises, bonds are getting more expensive and yields are falling.

## Accrued interest and settlement

Between coupon dates the seller is owed part of the next coupon. The buyer pays the clean price (чистая цена) plus accrued interest (накопленный купонный доход, НКД). The total is the dirty price (грязная цена). Russian brokers show NKD separately on every quote.

## How bonds are talked about

"OFZ yields rose 30 bps along the curve after the hawkish (жёсткое, «ястребиное») statement." "The issuer placed a three-year bond at a coupon of 15.5%, 150 bps over OFZ." "Investors extended duration (увеличили дюрацию) ahead of the expected rate cut." Hawkish means favouring higher rates; dovish (мягкое, «голубиное») means favouring lower rates.

> **Remember.** Price and yield move in opposite directions. Duration = sensitivity to rates. Spread over the risk-free rate = payment for credit risk. YTM is the bond's IRR.
