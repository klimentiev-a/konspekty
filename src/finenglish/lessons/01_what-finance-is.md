## Theory

## Three ideas behind everything

Finance (финансы) is the study of how people, companies and governments get money, spend it, and move it through time. Almost every topic in this course comes from three simple ideas.

**First: money has a time value (временная стоимость денег).** A rouble today is worth more than a rouble next year, because today you can invest it (инвестировать), earn interest (проценты) and have more than a rouble next year. So when we compare money in different years, we must adjust (скорректировать) it. This idea is called discounting (дисконтирование) and we will study it in Topic 6.

**Second: risk needs a reward (риск требует вознаграждения).** If two investments promise the same return (доходность), but one is riskier, nobody will choose the risky one. To attract investors (инвесторы), a risky asset (актив) must offer a higher expected return (ожидаемая доходность). This extra return is called a risk premium (премия за риск).

**Third: the price of an asset is the present value of what it will give you.** A share (акция) is worth the cash it will pay its owner in the future, discounted to today. The same is true for a bond (облигация), a building or a whole company. This is why finance people spend so much time forecasting cash flows (денежные потоки): the forecast is the price.

> **Remember.** Time, risk, and future cash flows. If you understand how these three connect, you understand the logic of finance.

## Who does what in finance

The industry (отрасль) is large, so here is a short map.

| Area | What they do | Typical employer |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate finance (корпоративные финансы) | Decide how a company raises money and what to invest in | Any large company, the CFO's team |
| Investment banking, IB (инвестиционный банкинг) | Advise on mergers and acquisitions, help companies issue shares and bonds | Investment banks, boutiques |
| Asset management (управление активами) | Invest other people's money in portfolios | Funds, pension funds, insurers |
| Sales and trading (продажи и торговля) | Buy and sell securities for clients and the bank itself | Banks, brokers |
| Private equity, PE (прямые инвестиции) | Buy whole companies, improve them, sell them later | PE funds |
| Equity research (аналитика по акциям) | Write reports and recommendations on listed companies | Banks, independent firms |

The chief financial officer, CFO (финансовый директор) manages money inside a company. An analyst (аналитик) and an associate (ассоциат, младший сотрудник) are the first two job titles in a bank. A managing director, MD (управляющий директор) is a senior banker who brings in clients.

## Two sides of a company

Every company can be described from two sides. On one side are its assets (активы): factories, inventory (запасы), cash (денежные средства), brands. On the other side is the money that paid for those assets. That money comes from two sources: debt (долг, заёмный капитал), which must be repaid with interest, and equity (собственный капитал), which belongs to the owners, the shareholders (акционеры).

The split between debt and equity is called the capital structure (структура капитала). Debt is cheaper, because interest is tax-deductible (вычитается из налоговой базы) and lenders (кредиторы) take less risk. But too much debt can push a company into default (дефолт, неплатёж) and bankruptcy (банкротство). Finding the right balance is one of the main jobs of corporate finance.

## Primary and secondary markets

When a company sells new shares or bonds to investors for the first time, this happens on the primary market (первичный рынок). The most famous example is an initial public offering, IPO (первичное публичное размещение акций). After that, investors trade those securities (ценные бумаги) with each other on the secondary market (вторичный рынок), for example on a stock exchange (фондовая биржа) such as the Moscow Exchange, MOEX (Московская биржа) or the New York Stock Exchange, NYSE (Нью-Йоркская фондовая биржа).

The company does not receive money from secondary-market trading. But the secondary market matters a lot: it sets the market price (рыночная цена), and that price tells the company how cheaply it can raise new money.

## Return, yield and growth

Three words look similar but mean different things.

- **Return (доходность, отдача)** is how much you earned on an investment over a period, usually in percent: price change plus any income.
- **Yield (доходность, обычно по облигациям или дивидендам)** is the income an asset pays relative to its price, for example a dividend yield (дивидендная доходность) of 8% or a bond yield (доходность облигации) of 15%.
- **Growth (рост)** is how fast something increases: revenue growth (рост выручки), earnings growth (рост прибыли), GDP growth (рост ВВП).

A classic mistake is to mix yield and return. A bond with a 15% yield can give a negative return in a year if its price falls.

## Nominal and real

Prices in finance can be nominal (номинальный), that is, in today's money without adjusting for inflation (инфляция), or real (реальный), adjusted for inflation. If your deposit (вклад) pays 14% and inflation is 7%, your real return is about 7%. Central banks (центральные банки) think in real terms when they set the key rate (ключевая ставка). We come back to this in Topic 19.

## Why finance people love ratios

A number alone says little. Revenue of 100 billion roubles is huge for a start-up (стартап) and small for an oil major (крупная нефтяная компания). That is why finance uses ratios (коэффициенты, мультипликаторы): profit divided by revenue, debt divided by earnings, price divided by earnings. A ratio makes companies of different sizes comparable (сопоставимыми). Most of this course is about which ratios matter and how to read them.

> **Remember.** Always ask three questions about a number: compared with what, over what period, and in which currency and prices (nominal or real).
