## Theory

## The players

A financial market (финансовый рынок) brings together those who have money and those who need it. The main participants (участники):

- **Issuers (эмитенты):** companies and governments that sell securities (ценные бумаги).
- **Investors (инвесторы):** retail investors (частные, розничные инвесторы), ordinary people with brokerage accounts, and institutional investors (институциональные инвесторы): pension funds (пенсионные фонды), insurance companies (страховые компании), mutual funds (паевые фонды), hedge funds (хедж-фонды), sovereign wealth funds (суверенные фонды).
- **Intermediaries (посредники):** brokers (брокеры), who execute orders for clients; dealers (дилеры), who trade for their own account; investment banks (инвестиционные банки), which do both and advise.
- **Infrastructure (инфраструктура):** exchanges, clearing houses, depositories.
- **Regulators (регуляторы):** in Russia the Bank of Russia (Банк России, ЦБ) supervises the whole financial market; in the US the Securities and Exchange Commission, SEC (Комиссия по ценным бумагам и биржам) oversees securities and the Federal Reserve (ФРС) oversees banks.

## The exchange and what happens after a trade

A stock exchange (фондовая биржа) is a marketplace with rules where buyers and sellers meet. Today it is a computer that runs an order book (биржевой стакан, книга заявок): a list of buy orders, bids (заявки на покупку), and sell orders, asks or offers (заявки на продажу), sorted by price. When a bid and an ask match, a trade (сделка) is executed (исполняется). The difference between the best bid and the best ask is the bid-ask spread (спред).

After the trade come clearing (клиринг) and settlement (расчёты). The clearing house, or central counterparty, CCP (центральный контрагент) becomes the buyer to every seller and the seller to every buyer, guaranteeing that the trade will be completed even if one party fails. Settlement is the actual exchange of securities for money; on the Moscow Exchange stocks settle on T+1, one business day after the trade. The central securities depository, CSD (центральный депозитарий; в России НРД, Национальный расчётный депозитарий) keeps the records of who owns what.

The Moscow Exchange Group (Московская биржа) owns the exchange, the clearing house (NCC, НКЦ) and the depository (NSD, НРД), and earns fees (комиссии) on every trade plus interest on client balances, which is why its profit rises when the key rate is high.

## Order types

- **Market order (рыночная заявка):** buy or sell now at the best available price. Fast, but in a thin market the price can be bad.
- **Limit order (лимитная заявка):** buy no higher, or sell no lower, than a stated price. May never execute.
- **Stop order, stop-loss (стоп-заявка, стоп-лосс):** becomes a market order when the price reaches a trigger level; used to limit losses.
- **Take-profit (тейк-профит):** the opposite, to lock in a gain.
- **Iceberg order (айсберг-заявка):** shows only part of the size, hiding a large trade.

## Liquidity and market makers

Liquidity (ликвидность) is the ability to buy or sell quickly without moving the price much. A liquid stock has a tight spread, deep order book (глубокий стакан) and high turnover (оборот). An illiquid (неликвидный) one has wide spreads and jumps on small orders. Liquidity is why big investors stick to large caps and why the free float matters.

A market maker (маркетмейкер) is a firm that continuously quotes bid and ask prices and earns the spread. Exchanges pay or require market makers to keep small stocks tradable. High-frequency trading, HFT (высокочастотная торговля) firms are the modern market makers, trading in microseconds with algorithms (алгоритмы). Algorithmic trading (алгоритмическая торговля) is now most of the volume in developed markets.

## Trading sessions and halts

The Moscow Exchange runs a main session (основная сессия) from 10:00 to 18:50 Moscow time, plus morning and evening sessions (утренняя и вечерняя сессии). The closing price (цена закрытия) is set by a closing auction (аукцион закрытия). If a stock moves too fast, the exchange may impose a trading halt (приостановка торгов) or a discrete auction (дискретный аукцион) to calm the market; US exchanges use circuit breakers (автоматические остановки торгов) when the index falls 7%, 13% or 20% in a day.

## OTC, dark pools and the money market

Not everything trades on an exchange. Over-the-counter, OTC (внебиржевой рынок) trading happens directly between dealers: most bonds, currencies and derivatives trade OTC. Dark pools (тёмные пулы) are private venues where large orders are matched without showing them to the market. The money market (денежный рынок) is the market for short-term borrowing, under a year: repo (репо, сделка с обратным выкупом), where a bank sells securities and buys them back tomorrow, is the main instrument; the RUONIA rate (ставка RUONIA) is the average overnight rate at which Russian banks lend to each other, and floaters are often tied to it.

## Rules of the game

Insider trading (инсайдерская торговля), buying or selling on non-public information, is illegal everywhere. Market manipulation (манипулирование рынком), for example pump and dump (разгон и сброс) of a small stock in Telegram channels, is also illegal and the Bank of Russia prosecutes it. Public companies must disclose (раскрывать) material information (существенная информация) to everyone at the same time. Rules about who can buy what are called investor protection (защита инвесторов): in Russia, a qualified investor (квалифицированный инвестор) status is required for complex or foreign instruments, and unqualified investors must pass a test (тестирование).

Know-your-customer, KYC (идентификация клиента) and anti-money-laundering, AML (противодействие отмыванию денег) rules make brokers check who their clients are and where the money comes from.

> **Remember.** Exchange matches orders, clearing house guarantees them, depository records ownership. Market order = now at any price, limit order = at my price or better. Liquidity = tight spread and depth. Insider trading is a crime.
