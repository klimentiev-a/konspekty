## Theory

## What a share is

A share, also called a stock (акция; в США чаще stock, в Британии share) is a piece of ownership in a company. If a company has 1,000 shares and you own 10, you own 1% of it. Shareholders (акционеры) have two rights: a share of the profit, paid as dividends (дивиденды), and a vote (голос) at the general meeting (общее собрание акционеров), where they elect the board of directors (совет директоров).

Common shares (обыкновенные акции) carry votes and receive what is left after everyone else is paid. Preferred shares (привилегированные акции, «префы») usually have no vote but a fixed or priority dividend, and stand before common shares if the company is liquidated (ликвидируется). In Russia, Sberbank and Surgutneftegas have both types; the preferred shares of Surgutneftegas are famous for their dividends tied to foreign-exchange gains.

## Why share prices move

In the long run a share is worth the present value of the cash it will bring (Topic 8). In the short run the price moves on news (новости), earnings releases (отчёты о результатах), interest rates (процентные ставки), and sentiment (настроение рынка). Analysts say a stock rallies (растёт, «ралли») or sells off (распродаётся), that the market is bullish (бычий, растущий) or bearish (медвежий, падающий). A correction (коррекция) is a fall of about 10% from the peak; a bear market (медвежий рынок) is a fall of 20% or more.

## Market capitalization

Market capitalization, market cap (рыночная капитализация) = share price × shares outstanding (акции в обращении). Companies are grouped as large-cap (крупная капитализация), mid-cap and small-cap. Free float (акции в свободном обращении) is the share of stock available to public investors, excluding the state, founders and strategic holders (стратегические акционеры). Low free float means low liquidity (ликвидность) and bigger price swings. Index providers weight companies by free-float market cap.

## Primary market: IPO and SPO

An initial public offering, IPO (первичное публичное размещение) is the first sale of shares to the public, after which the company is listed (котируется) on an exchange. Investment banks act as underwriters (андеррайтеры): they organise the deal, build the order book (книга заявок) in a process called bookbuilding (сбор заявок), and set the offer price (цена размещения). The prospectus (проспект эмиссии) describes the company and its risks. A lock-up (период запрета на продажу) stops insiders from selling for several months after the IPO.

A secondary public offering, SPO (вторичное размещение) is a later sale of existing shares by current owners; a follow-on offering (дополнительная эмиссия) sells new shares and brings money to the company. Many Russian IPOs since 2023 are small by global standards, but the vocabulary is the same.

## Dividends and buybacks

A dividend (дивиденд) is a cash payment to shareholders, usually once or several times a year. Important dates: the record date (дата закрытия реестра), when you must own the shares to receive the dividend, and the ex-dividend date (экс-дивидендная дата), the first day the stock trades without the dividend. On that day the price drops by roughly the dividend amount: the dividend gap (дивидендный гэп). The dividend yield (дивидендная доходность) = dividend per share, DPS (дивиденд на акцию) / price. Russian stocks are known for high yields, often 8–15%.

A share buyback (обратный выкуп) is the company buying its own shares on the market. It returns cash to shareholders indirectly: fewer shares mean higher EPS and often a higher price. Shares bought back become treasury shares (казначейские акции) and may be cancelled (погашены).

## Stock indices

A stock index (фондовый индекс) tracks a basket of shares. The MOEX Russia Index, IMOEX (индекс Мосбиржи) covers the largest Russian companies in roubles; the RTS Index (индекс РТС) is the same basket in dollars. In the US: the S&P 500 (500 largest companies), the Dow Jones (30 industrial names), the Nasdaq Composite (technology-heavy). Europe: FTSE 100, DAX, CAC 40. Asia: Nikkei 225, Hang Seng. Index funds and exchange-traded funds, ETFs (биржевые фонды) copy an index cheaply; this is passive investing (пассивное инвестирование), as opposed to active (активное).

## Growth, value and other styles

Investors sort stocks into styles. Growth stocks (акции роста) are companies expected to grow fast; they trade at high multiples and pay little or no dividend: technology, biotech. Value stocks (акции стоимости) are cheap relative to earnings or assets: banks, oil, utilities. Blue chips (голубые фишки) are large, stable, well-known companies. Defensive stocks (защитные акции) such as utilities and food retailers hold up in a downturn; cyclical stocks (циклические акции) such as metals and cars follow the economy. A rotation (ротация) is money moving from one style or sector to another.

## Long, short and leverage

To go long (купить, открыть длинную позицию) means to buy and hope the price rises. To go short (зашортить, открыть короткую позицию) means to borrow shares, sell them, and buy them back cheaper later; a short seller (шортист) profits from a fall. Margin trading (маржинальная торговля) uses borrowed money from the broker; a margin call (маржин-колл) is the broker's demand to add money when losses grow.

## Reading a quote

A quote (котировка) shows the last price, the change in percent, the bid (цена спроса, покупка) and the ask (цена предложения, продажа), the spread (спред) between them, and the volume (объём торгов). A ticker (тикер) is the short code: SBER, GAZP, AAPL. "Sber closed up 1.2% at 310 roubles on heavy volume" is a typical market report sentence.

> **Remember.** A share = ownership + dividend + vote. Market cap = price × shares. IPO is the first sale, SPO a later one. Dividends and buybacks return cash. Bull = up, bear = down.
