## Theory

## What private equity does

Private equity, PE (прямые инвестиции, фонды прямых инвестиций) is the business of buying whole companies, or large stakes, that are not traded on an exchange, holding them for 3–7 years, improving them, and selling at a profit. The money comes from a fund (фонд) raised from limited partners, LPs (ограниченные партнёры, инвесторы фонда): pension funds, endowments, insurers, wealthy families. The fund is managed by the general partner, GP (управляющий партнёр), the PE firm itself. Famous names: KKR, Blackstone, Carlyle, Apollo; in Russia, the market is small and dominated by local groups and family offices.

The GP charges a management fee (комиссия за управление), usually 2% of committed capital, and carried interest, carry (вознаграждение за успех), usually 20% of profits above a hurdle rate (пороговая доходность) of about 8%. This is the "2 and 20" model.

## The leveraged buyout

A leveraged buyout, LBO (выкуп с использованием заёмных средств) is the main tool of private equity: buying a company mostly with borrowed money, where the debt is secured (обеспечен) by the company's own assets and repaid from its own cash flows. The fund puts in equity (собственные средства фонда) of 30–50% of the price; banks and bond investors lend the rest.

The logic is like buying a flat with a mortgage: if the flat rises 20% in value and you paid 30% down, your equity has risen almost 70%. Leverage magnifies returns, and also losses.

## What makes a good LBO target

- **Stable, predictable cash flows (стабильные, предсказуемые денежные потоки)** to service the debt: no cyclical swings.
- **Low existing debt**, so there is room to add more.
- **Hard assets (материальные активы)** that can serve as collateral (обеспечение, залог).
- **Low capex needs**, so cash goes to debt repayment.
- **Room for improvement:** costs to cut, a weak management team to replace, non-core assets (непрофильные активы) to sell.
- **A clear exit (выход)** in a few years.

Mature businesses in boring industries are ideal: packaging, retail chains, business services. Fast-growing tech companies with losses are not, which is why they go to venture capital, VC (венчурный капитал) instead.

## Sources and uses

Every LBO model starts with a sources and uses table (таблица источников и использования средств).

| Uses (куда идут деньги) | | Sources (откуда деньги) | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purchase of equity | 800 | Senior debt (bank loans) | 450 |
| Refinancing of existing debt | 150 | Subordinated debt / mezzanine | 150 |
| Transaction fees | 50 | Sponsor equity | 400 |
| **Total** | **1,000** | **Total** | **1,000** |

Senior debt (старший долг) is repaid first and is cheapest; it is usually bank term loans (срочные кредиты) secured on assets. Subordinated or junior debt (субординированный, младший долг), high-yield bonds and mezzanine (мезонин), a hybrid of debt and equity, are riskier and more expensive. The PE fund is called the sponsor (спонсор сделки). Total leverage of 4–6x EBITDA is typical in the US and Europe; in Russia, with high rates, far less.

## Where the return comes from

Over the holding period (период владения) the sponsor earns from three sources:

1. **Debt paydown (погашение долга, делеверидж):** the company's cash flow repays debt, so the equity share of the same enterprise value grows. This is the classic LBO engine.
2. **EBITDA growth (рост EBITDA):** operational improvements (операционные улучшения), bolt-on acquisitions (дополняющие поглощения), price increases. The modern PE story is "value creation", not just financial engineering (финансовый инжиниринг).
3. **Multiple expansion (рост мультипликатора):** selling at a higher EV/EBITDA than the entry multiple, for example because the market is hotter. The least reliable source; a good model assumes exit multiple equals entry multiple.

## Exit

The exit (выход из инвестиции) can be a sale to a strategic buyer (trade sale, продажа стратегу), a sale to another PE fund (secondary buyout, вторичный выкуп), an IPO, or a dividend recapitalization (дивидендная рекапитализация), where the company borrows again to pay the sponsor a large dividend without a sale.

## Returns: IRR and MOIC

PE measures success with two numbers. Internal rate of return, IRR (внутренняя норма доходности) is the annualised return; funds target 20–25%. Multiple of invested capital, MOIC, also called money multiple or cash-on-cash (мультипликатор вложенного капитала) is how many times the equity was returned: 400 in, 1,000 out is 2.5x. The two must be read together: 2.5x in three years is a great IRR; 2.5x in ten years is not.

Quick mental rule: 2x in 3 years ≈ 26% IRR; 3x in 5 years ≈ 25% IRR; 2x in 5 years ≈ 15% IRR.

## A sketch of an LBO model

Entry: EBITDA 100, entry multiple 8x, EV 800, debt 500, equity 300. Over five years EBITDA grows to 130 and debt is paid down to 250. Exit at 8x: EV 1,040, minus debt 250 = equity 790. MOIC = 790 / 300 = 2.6x, IRR ≈ 21%. The model then runs sensitivities (чувствительность) on exit multiple, EBITDA growth and leverage.

## Risks and criticism

Leverage cuts both ways: a recession can push an LBO company into default (дефолт) and restructuring (реструктуризация), as happened to many retailers. Critics say PE loads companies with debt, cuts jobs and takes fees. Supporters say PE forces discipline and that the returns, net of fees, have beaten public markets for decades. Both are partly right.

> **Remember.** LBO = buy with mostly borrowed money, repay from the company's cash flow, sell in 3–7 years. Returns come from debt paydown, EBITDA growth and multiple expansion. Measure with IRR and MOIC together.
