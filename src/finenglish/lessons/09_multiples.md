## Theory

## Relative valuation

A multiple (мультипликатор) is a ratio of a company's value to one of its financial metrics (финансовый показатель). Valuing a company with multiples is called relative valuation (сравнительная оценка): we assume that similar companies should trade at similar multiples, so we take the multiple of comparable companies (сопоставимые компании, аналоги) and apply it to our target (целевая компания).

If peers (аналоги) trade at 6x EBITDA and our company has EBITDA of 100, its enterprise value should be about 600. Simple, fast, and based on real market prices, which is why multiples are used more often than DCF in daily work.

## The golden rule: match the numerator and the denominator

Every multiple has a value in the numerator (числитель) and a metric in the denominator (знаменатель). They must belong to the same group of investors.

- **Enterprise value, EV (стоимость предприятия)** belongs to all investors, so it goes with metrics before interest: revenue, EBITDA, EBIT.
- **Equity value, market capitalization (рыночная капитализация)** belongs to shareholders only, so it goes with metrics after interest: net income, book value of equity.

EV / Net income is wrong. Price / EBITDA is wrong. Interviewers check this.

## The main multiples

| Multiple | Formula | When used |
|---|---|---|
| EV/EBITDA | Enterprise value / EBITDA | The default for most industries; ignores capital structure and D&A policy |
| EV/EBIT | Enterprise value / EBIT | When capex intensity differs between peers |
| EV/Sales (EV/Revenue) | Enterprise value / Revenue | Loss-making or early-stage companies |
| P/E (price-to-earnings) | Share price / EPS, or Market cap / Net income | The most quoted multiple for stocks; needs positive earnings |
| P/B (price-to-book) | Market cap / Book value of equity | Banks and insurers, where assets are financial |
| EV/Capacity | EV / tonnes, barrels, megawatts | Industry-specific: steel, oil, power |
| Dividend yield | Dividend per share / Price | Income investors, Russian market |

P/E (коэффициент цена/прибыль) is read like this: a P/E of 10 means investors pay 10 roubles for each rouble of annual profit, or that the investment "pays back" in 10 years at current earnings. Its inverse, earnings yield (доходность по прибыли), 10%, is handy for comparing with bond yields.

## Forward and trailing

Multiples can use last twelve months, LTM or trailing (за последние двенадцать месяцев, трейлинг), or next twelve months, NTM or forward (прогнозные, форвардные). Forward multiples are usually lower for growing companies, because next year's earnings are higher. Always compare like with like: forward with forward.

## What drives a multiple

A high multiple is not "expensive" by itself. Multiples are shorthand for a DCF: EV/EBITDA is high when growth (рост) is high, risk (риск) is low, and the company needs little capex to grow. From the Gordon model, P/E ≈ payout / (Ke − g). So a company with higher growth or lower cost of equity deserves a higher multiple. When a stock trades at a premium (с премией) to peers, ask why: better growth, better margins, or just hype.

## Trading comps: comparable company analysis

Comparable company analysis, trading comps (анализ сопоставимых публичных компаний) uses the multiples of listed peers.

1. **Select the peer group (группа аналогов):** same industry, similar size, geography and business model. For a Russian steelmaker: Severstal, NLMK, MMK, plus global names with adjustments.
2. **Gather the data:** EV, EBITDA, net income, forecasts from consensus (консенсус-прогноз аналитиков).
3. **Calculate the multiples** for each peer; look at the median (медиана) and the range, not just the average, because one outlier (выброс) can distort the mean.
4. **Apply** the multiple range to the target's metric to get an implied valuation (подразумеваемая оценка).

Trading comps show what the market pays today for a minority stake (миноритарный пакет) in a similar company.

## Precedent transactions

Precedent transaction analysis, transaction comps (анализ прошлых сделок) uses multiples paid in actual acquisitions (поглощения) of similar companies. These multiples are usually higher, because a buyer pays a control premium (премия за контроль), often 20–40%, to get the whole company and its synergies (синергии). Precedent transactions answer "what would a buyer pay?", trading comps answer "what does the market pay?". Both are shown on the football field together with the DCF.

## Adjustments and pitfalls

- **Normalise (нормализуйте) the metrics:** remove one-off items from EBITDA and net income.
- **Check the definition of debt:** include leases and pension liabilities consistently.
- **Mind the currency and the date:** market data changes every day.
- **Negative or tiny earnings** make P/E meaningless; use EV/Sales or EV/EBITDA.
- **Cyclical industries (циклические отрасли)** such as metals look cheap at the peak of the cycle, when earnings are high, and expensive at the bottom. Use mid-cycle (среднециклические) earnings or EV/Capacity.
- **Different accounting standards** across countries change EBITDA; IFRS 16 raised EBITDA for lease-heavy companies.

## How multiples are quoted in conversation

"The stock trades at 5 times forward EBITDA." "Peers are at 12x earnings." "They paid 8x for the asset." "Russian equities trade at a steep discount (с большим дисконтом) to emerging-market peers." Note the word "times" (раз) and the letter x after the number.

> **Remember.** EV goes with EBITDA, EBIT and revenue; price goes with earnings and book value. High multiple = high growth or low risk. Trading comps for the market price, precedent transactions for the price with a control premium.
