## Theory

## Why investors read macro

Macroeconomics (макроэкономика) is the study of the whole economy: growth, prices, employment, interest rates, exchange rates. Markets react to macro data every day, because it changes two inputs of every valuation: expected cash flows and the discount rate. A rate hike (повышение ставки) raises discount rates and lowers asset prices; a recession (рецессия) lowers cash flows.

## GDP and the business cycle

Gross domestic product, GDP (валовой внутренний продукт, ВВП) is the value of everything produced in a country in a year. Real GDP growth (реальный рост ВВП) is growth after inflation. GDP per capita (ВВП на душу населения) compares living standards; at purchasing power parity, PPP (по паритету покупательной способности) it adjusts for price levels between countries.

The business cycle (экономический цикл) runs through expansion (подъём), peak (пик), contraction or recession (спад, рецессия), and trough (дно). A technical recession is two consecutive quarters (два квартала подряд) of falling GDP. Leading indicators (опережающие индикаторы) such as the purchasing managers' index, PMI (индекс деловой активности), new orders and the yield curve move before GDP; lagging indicators (запаздывающие индикаторы) such as unemployment (безработица) move after. "Soft landing" (мягкая посадка) means slowing inflation without a recession; "hard landing" (жёсткая посадка) means a recession.

## Inflation

Inflation (инфляция) is the general rise in prices; the consumer price index, CPI (индекс потребительских цен, ИПЦ) measures it for a basket of goods. Core inflation (базовая инфляция) excludes volatile food and energy prices. Headline inflation (общая инфляция) includes everything. Economists distinguish demand-pull inflation (инфляция спроса), when the economy overheats (перегревается), and cost-push inflation (инфляция издержек), when input prices rise, for example after a currency devaluation. Disinflation (дезинфляция) is slowing inflation; deflation (дефляция) is falling prices, which sounds nice but is dangerous because people delay spending. Inflation expectations (инфляционные ожидания), measured by surveys, matter because they become self-fulfilling.

## Central banks and the key rate

A central bank (центральный банк) controls the money supply (денежная масса) and sets the policy rate, in Russia the key rate (ключевая ставка), in the US the federal funds rate (ставка по федеральным фондам). Most central banks follow inflation targeting (таргетирование инфляции): the Bank of Russia targets 4%, the Fed and the ECB 2%. When inflation is above target, they tighten (ужесточают политику) by raising rates; when the economy weakens, they ease (смягчают) by cutting. A hawk (ястреб) favours high rates, a dove (голубь) low rates. Forward guidance (сигнал о будущей политике) is the central bank's hint about future moves.

The transmission mechanism (трансмиссионный механизм) is how the rate reaches the economy: higher key rate → higher deposit and loan rates → less borrowing and more saving → less demand → slower price growth. It takes 3–6 quarters, which is why central banks act in advance. Other tools: reserve requirements (нормативы резервирования), open market operations (операции на открытом рынке), quantitative easing, QE (количественное смягчение), where the central bank buys bonds to push long rates down, and quantitative tightening, QT (количественное ужесточение), the reverse.

Real interest rate (реальная процентная ставка) = nominal rate − inflation. With a key rate of 14% and inflation of 7%, the real rate is 7%, extremely tight by world standards; that is the Bank of Russia's choice for fighting inflation after 2023.

## Exchange rates

The exchange rate (валютный курс) is the price of one currency in another. A currency appreciates (укрепляется) or depreciates (ослабевает); a sharp official cut is a devaluation (девальвация). What moves it: interest rate differentials (разница процентных ставок), because high rates attract capital (carry trade, керри-трейд); the current account (счёт текущих операций), exports minus imports plus income flows; capital flows (потоки капитала); and risk sentiment. For Russia the main driver is the trade balance (торговый баланс): oil and gas export revenues against import demand, plus capital controls (ограничения на движение капитала) after 2022. A floating exchange rate (плавающий курс) is set by the market; a fixed or pegged rate (фиксированный курс, привязка) is held by the central bank through interventions (валютные интервенции). The Bank of Russia floats the rouble but buys and sells yuan under the budget rule (бюджетное правило).

A strong rouble helps importers and consumers but hurts exporters and the budget; a weak rouble does the opposite. That is why oil companies' shares rise when the rouble falls.

## Fiscal policy

Fiscal policy (бюджетная, фискальная политика) is government spending and taxes. A budget deficit (дефицит бюджета) is spending above revenue, financed by borrowing: in Russia through OFZ. Public debt (государственный долг) is the accumulated total; Russia's is small, about 15–20% of GDP, while Japan's exceeds 200% and the US is above 120%. Large deficits push up bond yields and can feed inflation, so fiscal and monetary policy (денежно-кредитная политика) pull in opposite directions when the government spends heavily and the central bank fights inflation.

## How macro moves markets

| Event | Bonds | Stocks | Currency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rate hike | Prices fall, yields rise | Fall, especially growth and indebted companies | Strengthens |
| Rate cut | Prices rise | Rise | Weakens |
| Inflation above forecast | Fall (more hikes expected) | Mixed, usually down | Mixed |
| Strong GDP data | Fall (hikes more likely) | Rise | Strengthens |
| Oil price jump | Russia: rouble bonds fine | Oil stocks up, airlines down | Rouble strengthens |

The calendar: the Bank of Russia's board meets eight times a year, the Fed's FOMC (Федеральный комитет по операциям на открытом рынке) eight times; inflation data come weekly in Russia and monthly in the US (CPI), and the US jobs report, non-farm payrolls (данные по занятости вне сельского хозяйства), lands on the first Friday of the month.

> **Remember.** Central bank fights inflation with the key rate; the real rate is nominal minus inflation. Higher rates: bonds down, stocks down, currency up. For the rouble, watch oil and the trade balance. Fiscal deficits push yields up.
