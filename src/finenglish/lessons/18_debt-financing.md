## Theory

## Why companies borrow

Debt (долг, заёмный капитал) is cheaper than equity: lenders take less risk and interest is tax-deductible (Topic 7). Borrowing also lets owners keep control (контроль), because lenders get no votes. The price is obligation: interest must be paid and principal (основная сумма долга) repaid whatever happens to the business. A company's debt capacity (долговая ёмкость) is how much it can borrow safely, usually judged by Net debt / EBITDA and interest coverage (Topic 10).

## Types of debt

A bilateral loan (двусторонний кредит) is from one bank; a club deal (клубная сделка) from a few; a syndicated loan (синдицированный кредит) from many. In Russia, bank loans still dominate corporate borrowing, and the bond market is growing, especially floaters (флоатеры).

## Interest: fixed or floating

A fixed rate (фиксированная ставка) stays the same for the life of the loan. A floating rate (плавающая ставка) is a reference rate plus a margin (маржа, спред): in Russia, key rate + 3%, or RUONIA + spread; internationally, SOFR for dollars and EURIBOR for euros replaced the old LIBOR. Floating-rate borrowers suffer when the central bank raises rates; in 2024–2025 many Russian companies saw interest costs double. Interest rate swaps (Topic 13) can convert floating to fixed.

## Security and seniority

Secured debt (обеспеченный долг) is backed by collateral (залог, обеспечение): property, equipment, shares, receivables. If the borrower defaults, the lender can seize and sell the collateral. Unsecured debt (необеспеченный долг) relies only on the borrower's promise and gets paid after secured creditors.

Seniority (старшинство, очерёдность) is the order of repayment in bankruptcy (банкротство): senior secured first, then senior unsecured, then subordinated (субординированный), then preferred shares, then common shareholders, who usually get nothing. This order is called the waterfall (водопад выплат). A guarantee (поручительство, гарантия) from a parent company (материнская компания) makes a subsidiary's debt safer.

## Covenants

A covenant (ковенант) is a promise in the loan agreement. Financial covenants (финансовые ковенанты) are the important ones: keep Net debt / EBITDA below 3.5x, keep interest coverage above 3x, keep equity above a minimum. Negative covenants (запретительные ковенанты) forbid actions: no new debt above a limit, no dividends above a share of profit, no sale of key assets, no change of control (смена контроля) without consent. Positive covenants (обязывающие ковенанты) require things: audited accounts, insurance.

Breaking a covenant is a covenant breach (нарушение ковенанта). It is a technical default (технический дефолт) even if interest is paid on time, and lets lenders demand early repayment (досрочное погашение). Usually they negotiate instead: a waiver (отказ от применения санкций) for a fee, or amended terms. Covenant-lite loans (кредиты с мягкими ковенантами) with few restrictions were common in the US LBO market before 2022.

## Credit ratings

A credit rating (кредитный рейтинг) is an independent opinion on the probability of default (вероятность дефолта). International agencies: S&P, Moody's, Fitch; Russian: ACRA (АКРА), Expert RA (Эксперт РА), NCR (НКР), NRA (НРА). Scales run from AAA (highest) to D (default). The line between BBB− and BB+ divides investment grade (инвестиционный уровень) from speculative grade (спекулятивный уровень). Many funds may only hold investment-grade paper, so a downgrade (понижение рейтинга) below the line, called becoming a fallen angel (падший ангел), forces selling and raises borrowing costs. The outlook (прогноз по рейтингу) is positive, stable or negative; a watch (пересмотр) means a change is likely soon.

## Refinancing and maturity profile

Refinancing (рефинансирование) means replacing old debt with new, usually at better terms or to extend maturities. The maturity profile or debt wall (график погашений, «стена погашений») shows how much is due each year; a big amount in one year is refinancing risk (риск рефинансирования), especially when markets are closed. Good treasurers (казначеи) spread maturities and keep undrawn credit lines (невыбранные кредитные линии) as a liquidity cushion (подушка ликвидности).

## Default and restructuring

A default (дефолт) is a missed payment. A grace period (льготный период) of a few days usually applies. After that, lenders can accelerate (потребовать досрочного погашения) the debt and start bankruptcy proceedings (процедура банкротства). More often, the parties negotiate a restructuring (реструктуризация): extending maturities (продление сроков), lowering the rate, a haircut (списание части долга) where lenders accept less than par, or a debt-for-equity swap (конвертация долга в акции) where lenders become shareholders. Distressed debt (проблемный долг) investors buy such bonds cheaply hoping to profit from the restructuring.

> **Remember.** Debt is cheap but mandatory. Secured before unsecured, senior before subordinated. Covenants are the lender's early-warning system. Rating = opinion on default risk. Spread maturities to avoid a debt wall.
