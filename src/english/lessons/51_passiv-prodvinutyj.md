# Урок 51. Пассив продвинутый: все времена, модальные, have something done
short: Passive во всех временах и с модальными, have/get something done, it is said that / he is said to, безличные конструкции
level: B1
unit: 5
theme: no
minutes: 60

## Цели
- Образовывать пассив во всех основных временах, включая Continuous, Perfect и будущие формы.
- Использовать пассив с модальными глаголами (must be done, should have been done) и в формах инфинитива и герундия.
- Строить конструкцию have / get something done и отличать её от «сделать самому».
- Пользоваться безличными конструкциями it is said that … и he is said to …, понимать новостной стиль.
- Знать, у каких глаголов пассива нет, и избегать ошибок вроде *it was happened*.
- Переводить русские безличные предложения («Мне сказали», «Здесь говорят по-английски», «Его уволили») на английский.

## Теория

### 1. Зачем нужен пассив и как он устроен
Пассив (страдательный залог) используют, когда **важнее действие или результат, чем тот, кто его выполнил**. Типичные причины:

1. Исполнитель неизвестен или неважен: *My bike **was stolen**.* — У меня украли велосипед.
2. Исполнитель очевиден: *The criminals **were arrested**.* (понятно, что полицией)
3. Нужен нейтральный, формальный стиль (новости, инструкции, научные тексты): *The results **will be announced** on Friday.*
4. Мы хотим поставить в начало предложения то, о чём говорим: *The Eiffel Tower **was designed** by Gustave Eiffel's company.*

Общая формула: **be (в нужном времени) + третья форма глагола (V3)**. Исполнитель вводится словом **by** и называется, только если это новая и важная информация: *The novel was written **by a teenager**.*

> **Запомни.** Время пассива определяет форма глагола **be**, а смысловой глагол всегда стоит в третьей форме: *is made, was made, has been made, will be made.*

### 2. Пассив во всех временах
| Время | Актив | Пассив |
|---|---|---|
| Present Simple | They **make** cars here. | Cars **are made** here. |
| Present Continuous | They **are repairing** the road. | The road **is being repaired**. |
| Past Simple | Someone **stole** my bag. | My bag **was stolen**. |
| Past Continuous | They **were painting** the house. | The house **was being painted**. |
| Present Perfect | They **have cancelled** the flight. | The flight **has been cancelled**. |
| Past Perfect | They **had closed** the shop. | The shop **had been closed**. |
| Future Simple | They **will finish** it soon. | It **will be finished** soon. |
| be going to | They **are going to build** a bridge. | A bridge **is going to be built**. |
| Future Perfect | They **will have sold** the house. | The house **will have been sold**. |

Что нужно помнить:
- **Continuous-пассив** (*is being done, was being done*) нужен для действия в процессе. Русские студенты часто пропускают *being*: ❌ *The room is cleaning now.* ✅ *The room is being cleaned now.*
- **Perfect Continuous в пассиве** (*has been being done*) звучит тяжело и почти не употребляется. Вместо него оставляют активный залог (*They have been repairing the road for weeks*) или говорят иначе (*The road has been under repair for weeks*). Замена на *The road has been repaired* меняет смысл: так говорят о законченном ремонте.
- **Вопросы:** вспомогательный глагол идёт перед подлежащим: *Was the window broken? When was it found? Is the car being repaired? Has the letter been sent?*
- **Вопрос к исполнителю:** *Who was the letter written by?* (в формальной речи *By whom was the letter written?*). Если спрашиваем о подлежащем, пассив не нужен: ✅ *Who wrote the letter?* ❌ *Who was wrote the letter?*
- **Отрицание:** *The report hasn't been finished. The shop wasn't being cleaned.*

### 3. Пассив с модальными глаголами
Схема для настоящего и будущего: **modal + be + V3**. Для прошлого: **modal + have been + V3**.

| Значение | Настоящее и будущее | Прошлое |
|---|---|---|
| необходимость | The form **must be filled** in. | The form **had to be filled** in. |
| совет, упрёк | Children **should be told** the truth. | He **should have been told**. |
| возможность | This problem **can be solved**. | It **could have been solved** earlier. |
| вывод (урок 45) | The door **must be locked**. | The window **must have been broken** at night. |
| невозможность | That **can't be done**. | The letter **can't have been sent**. |
| долженствование | It **has to be done** / **needs to be done**. | It **had to be done**. |

Примеры:
- *All passengers **must be checked** before boarding.* — Всех пассажиров нужно проверить перед посадкой.
- *The thief **can't have been seen** by anyone. The street was empty.* — Вора никто не мог заметить. Улица была пуста.
- *You **should have been warned**.* — Тебя следовало предупредить.

Пассив есть и у **инфинитива** и **герундия**:

| Форма | Образец | Пример |
|---|---|---|
| to be + V3 | to be done | *She wants to be invited.* |
| to have been + V3 | to have been done | *The bridge is said to have been built in 1200.* |
| being + V3 | being done | *I hate being told what to do.* |
| having been + V3 | having been done | *Having been warned, he stayed at home.* |

### 4. Особые случаи: два объекта, фразовые глаголы, глаголы без пассива
**Два дополнения.** С глаголами give, send, show, offer, tell, teach, buy, pay возможны две формы:
- *They gave **me** a book.* → ✅ ***I was given** a book.* (чаще) или *A book was given to me.*
- *Nobody told **me** the truth.* → *I wasn't told the truth.*

По-русски мы тоже говорим «мне сказали», поэтому английское *I was told* выглядит привычно. Но ❌ *I was said that …* — нельзя: у say нет дополнения-лица (говорят *say something **to** somebody*), поэтому человек не может стать подлежащим, как с tell. Говорим: *I was told that …* или *It was said that …* (Конструкция *He is said to be rich* — другое, она в разделе 6.)

**Предложные и фразовые глаголы** сохраняют предлог или частицу:
- *Her grandmother looked after her.* → *She **was looked after** by her grandmother.*
- *They called off the match.* → *The match **was called off**.*
- *Someone has slept in this bed.* → *This bed **has been slept in**.*

**Глаголы без пассива.** Пассив образуют только переходные глаголы (с прямым дополнением). Нельзя поставить в пассив:
- глаголы без дополнения: happen, arrive, appear, die, sleep, fall, rise, seem, occur. ❌ *The accident was happened.* ✅ *The accident happened.*
- глаголы состояния: have (в значении «иметь»), fit, suit, resemble, lack, cost, weigh. ❌ *A car is had by him.* ✅ *He has a car.*

Ещё одна русская ловушка — **be born**. Это всегда пассив, а о рождении конкретного человека говорят в Past Simple: ✅ *I was born in 1995.* ❌ *I born in 1995.* ❌ *I am born in 1995.* (В общих фактах бывает и настоящее: *Thousands of babies are born every day.*)

**By или with?** *By* называет исполнителя: *painted by Monet*. *With* называет инструмент или материал: *The window was broken with a stone. The room was filled with smoke.*

### 5. Have / get something done
Конструкция **have + объект + V3** означает, что **действие выполнил кто-то другой для нас**, обычно по нашей просьбе или за деньги (услуги).

- *I **had my hair cut** yesterday.* — Я вчера подстриглась (в парикмахерской).
- *We're **having the kitchen painted**.* — Нам красят кухню.
- *She **has her car serviced** every year.* — Ей каждый год обслуживают машину.
- *Where do you **have your hair cut**?* — Где ты стрижёшься?
- *I need to **get my passport renewed**.* — Мне нужно обновить паспорт.

Сравни: *I cut my hair* — я сама себя подстригла; *I had my hair cut* — меня подстригли.

Есть второе значение той же структуры: **неприятное событие**, которое случилось с человеком: *He **had his wallet stolen** on the bus. She **had her phone taken away**.* Исполнитель в обоих случаях часто не называется.

Нюансы:
- **get** более разговорный, чем have: *I got my laptop fixed.*
- Времена меняются по have: *I'll have it done. I've had my eyes tested. I'm going to get my teeth checked.* Вопросы и отрицания с do: *Did you have your car fixed? I didn't have it repaired.*
- **get something done** без услуги значит «успеть, закончить»: *I finally got the report finished.*
- **Get-пассив** (*get + V3*) употребляется в разговорной речи для событий, особенно неожиданных или неприятных: *He got fired. She got hurt. We got stuck in traffic.* Он не подходит для глаголов состояния, а исполнитель почти никогда не называется.

### 6. Безличный пассив: it is said that / he is said to
В новостях и официальных текстах часто встречаются конструкции с глаголами say, think, believe, know, report, expect, consider, understand, claim, estimate, announce. Есть два способа сказать одно и то же:

| Конструкция с it | Конструкция с подлежащим |
|---|---|
| *It is said that the company is losing money.* | *The company is said **to be losing** money.* |
| *It is believed that he left the country.* | *He is believed **to have left** the country.* |
| *It is expected that prices will rise.* | *Prices are expected **to rise**.* |
| *It was thought that the castle was haunted.* | *The castle was thought **to be** haunted.* |
| *It is reported that two people were injured.* | *Two people are reported **to have been** injured.* |

Выбор инфинитива зависит от времени события:
- одновременно с моментом сообщения: **to + V** или **to be + V-ing**: *He is said to be living in Spain.*
- **раньше** момента сообщения: **to have + V3**: *He is said to have lived in Spain.*
- о будущем: **to + V** с глаголами expect, plan: *The president is expected to arrive tomorrow.*

Типичные русские ошибки: ❌ *He is said that he is rich.* ❌ *He is said have left.* ✅ *He is said to be rich.* ✅ *He is said to have left.*

Близкая конструкция **be supposed to** выражает ожидание или обязанность: *You're supposed to wear a helmet. The train is supposed to arrive at nine, but it's late.* Для объявлений: *It has been announced that …, It was decided that …, It is recommended that you book early.*

### 7. Безличные русские предложения и английский пассив
В русском много предложений без подлежащего: «Мне сказали», «Здесь говорят по-английски», «Его уволили», «В кафе подают завтрак». В английском у предложения должно быть подлежащее, и часто для этого подходит пассив.

| Русский | Английский |
|---|---|
| Мне сказали, что вы придёте. | I was told that you would come. |
| Здесь говорят по-английски. | English is spoken here. |
| Его уволили. | He was fired. / He has been dismissed. |
| В кафе подают завтрак. | Breakfast is served in the café. |
| Меня попросили подождать. | I was asked to wait. |
| Нам не разрешили войти. | We weren't allowed to go in. |
| Говорят, что она богата. | She is said to be rich. |
| Здесь нельзя курить. | Smoking is not allowed here. |

Когда пассив **не стоит** использовать:
- когда исполнитель — «я» или «мы» и это важно: ❌ *The mistake was made by me.* ✅ *I made a mistake.*
- в живой речи, если пассив усложняет предложение: *I was given the news by the manager* лучше *The manager told me the news.*
- в длинных цепочках: ❌ *It is believed to have been decided to be postponed.*

### 8. Типичные ошибки русскоговорящих
| ❌ Ошибка | ✅ Правильно | Комментарий |
|---|---|---|
| *The accident was happened yesterday.* | *The accident happened yesterday.* | happen — без пассива |
| *The room is cleaning now.* | *The room is being cleaned now.* | нужно being |
| *The book writes by Orwell.* | *The book was written by Orwell.* | нужны be и V3 |
| *He was told me about it.* | *He told me about it. / I was told about it.* | один объект становится подлежащим |
| *I cut my hair yesterday* (в салоне) | *I had my hair cut yesterday.* | услуга |
| *She is said that she is ill.* | *She is said to be ill.* | to + V |
| *The window broke by the boy.* | *The window was broken by the boy.* | нужен пассив |
| *I am born in 2000.* | *I was born in 2000.* | о своём рождении — was / were born |

## Диалог
**Что происходит в офисе?**
- Emma: Have you seen the office? The whole place is being repainted. | Ты видел офис? Его целиком перекрашивают.
- Jack: I know. The meeting room was damaged last week, so it's being repaired too. | Знаю. Переговорную повредили на прошлой неделе, так что её тоже ремонтируют.
- Emma: How did it get damaged? | Как её повредили?
- Jack: A pipe burst. It's said that the building was built in the 1950s and the pipes were never replaced. | Лопнула труба. Говорят, что здание построили в 1950-х, а трубы так и не заменили.
- Emma: Typical! When will it all be finished? | Типично! Когда всё закончат?
- Jack: It's expected to be done by Friday, but I doubt it. | Ожидается, что к пятнице всё будет готово, но я сомневаюсь.

- Jack: Mine is being replaced. I had it checked yesterday, and the technician said it should have been replaced years ago. | Мой заменяют. Вчера я отдал его на проверку, и техник сказал, что его следовало заменить много лет назад.
- Emma: By the way, were you told about the meeting? It has been moved to Monday. | Кстати, тебе сказали про встречу? Её перенесли на понедельник.
- Jack: No, I wasn't told anything! Who announced it? | Нет, мне ничего не сказали! Кто об этом объявил?
- Emma: The manager. An email was sent to everyone this morning. | Менеджер. Сегодня утром всем отправили письмо.
- Jack: Then I must have been left off the list. | Значит, меня, должно быть, не внесли в список.

- Jack: I'm going to have my email address checked. | Я собираюсь попросить проверить мой адрес электронной почты.
- Emma: And apparently a new coffee machine is going to be installed next week. | А ещё, судя по всему, на следующей неделе установят новую кофемашину.

## Упражнения

### Упражнение 1. Выбери форму пассива
Выбери форму, которая подходит по смыслу. Только один вариант верен.
1. The road ___ at the moment. :: is repairing | is being repaired* | is repaired | has repaired
2. This church ___ in the twelfth century. :: built | was built* | has been built | is building
3. The match ___ because of the rain. :: has been cancelled* | has cancelled | was cancel | is cancelling
4. Our new office ___ by the mayor next month. :: will be opened* | will open | is opened | was opening
5. When I arrived, the room ___ . :: had been cleaned* | has been cleaned | was cleaning | had cleaned
6. Hundreds of cars ___ in this factory every year. :: produce | are produced* | have produced | is produced
7. The documents ___ when the boss walked in. :: were being signed* | were signing | had signed | signed
8. By next year, the bridge ___ . :: will have been finished* | will be finishing | has been finished | would finish

### Упражнение 2. Поставь глагол в пассив
Впиши нужную пассивную форму глагола в скобках (полную или сокращённую).
1. These cars ___ in Germany. (make; Present Simple) => are made
2. The film ___ in 2019. (release; Past Simple) => was released
3. The room ___ at the moment. (clean; Present Continuous) => is being cleaned
4. The report ___ by Friday. (finish; will) => will be finished
5. The thieves ___ already. (arrest; Present Perfect) => have been arrested / 've been arrested
6. When we got there, the match ___ . (cancel; Past Perfect) => had been cancelled / had been canceled
7. A new hospital ___ next year. (build; going to) => is going to be built
8. The children ___ by a neighbour when the fire started. (look after; Past Continuous) => were being looked after

### Упражнение 3. Пассив с модальными глаголами
Выбери верную форму.
1. This form ___ by Monday. :: must fill | must be filled* | must be filling | must been filled
2. The thief can't ___ by the camera. It was broken. :: have been seen* | be seen | has been seen | have seen
3. You should ___ about the change. Nobody told you! :: have been told* | be telling | has been told | have told
4. The problem could ___ easily if we had asked him. :: be solved | have solved | have been solved* | been solved
5. Children ___ to use the pool without an adult. :: aren't allowed* | don't allow | aren't allowing | not allowed
6. The door ___ at night. It's the rule. :: has to locked | has to be locked* | has to lock | have to be locked
7. I don't like ___ what to do. :: telling | to tell | being told* | to been told

### Упражнение 4. Перепиши так, чтобы смысл не изменился
Перепиши предложение целиком. Используй пассив или have / get something done, как указано.
1. They are building a new bridge. => A new bridge is being built.
2. Someone has stolen my bike. => My bike has been stolen.
3. People say that he is a spy. (He ...) => He is said to be a spy. / He's said to be a spy.
4. They believe that she left the country. (She ...) => She is believed to have left the country. / She's believed to have left the country.
5. The manager will announce the results tomorrow. => The results will be announced tomorrow. / The results will be announced tomorrow by the manager. / The results will be announced by the manager tomorrow.
6. A hairdresser cut Anna's hair yesterday. (Anna had ...) => Anna had her hair cut yesterday. / Anna had her hair cut by a hairdresser yesterday. / Anna had her hair cut yesterday by a hairdresser.
7. Nobody told me about the change. (I ...) => I wasn't told about the change. / I was not told about the change.
8. They didn't invite us to the party. (We ...) => We weren't invited to the party. / We were not invited to the party.
9. People expect that prices will rise. (Prices ...) => Prices are expected to rise.
10. A mechanic checked my car last week. (I had ...) => I had my car checked last week. / I had my car checked by a mechanic last week. / I had my car checked last week by a mechanic.

### Упражнение 5. Найди правильное предложение
Выбери предложение без ошибок.
1. Что верно? :: The accident was happened on Sunday. | The accident happened on Sunday.* | The accident has been happened on Sunday. | The accident was happening by Sunday.
2. Что верно? :: The room is cleaning now. | The room cleans now. | The room is being cleaned now.* | The room is cleaned by now.
3. Что верно? :: I born in 1998. | I am born in 1998. | I was born in 1998.* | I have been born in 1998.
4. Что верно? :: He was told me about the problem. | I was told about the problem.* | I told about the problem. | I was telling about the problem.
5. Что верно? :: She is said that she is ill. | She is said she is ill. | She is said to be ill.* | She is said being ill.

## Вместе
Эти задания удобно делать вдвоём — вслух, без конспекта.

**Задание 1. Выпуск новостей.** Один — диктор, другой — репортёр с места событий. Придумайте новость (пожар, ограбление, открытие музея). Диктор объявляет: *It is reported that …*, а репортёр уточняет: *The fire is believed to have started in the kitchen. Two people have been injured. The building is being searched.* Используйте минимум 6 пассивных конструкций, среди них хотя бы одну с is said to и одну с has been done.

**Задание 2. Услуги.** Вместе составьте список из 8 услуг (стрижка, ремонт часов, проверка зрения, покраска стен, чистка ковра, доставка пиццы, фото на паспорт, обновление телефона). По очереди спрашивайте: *When did you last have your hair cut? Where do you get your car serviced? What are you going to have done this month?* Отвечайте полными предложениями с have / get something done.

**Задание 3. Кто и что сделал?** Один называет известный факт активным предложением (*Leonardo painted the Mona Lisa*), второй превращает его в пассив (*The Mona Lisa was painted by Leonardo*) и придумывает вопрос в пассиве (*Who was the Mona Lisa painted by?*). Сделайте по 5 фактов каждому: изобретения, книги, здания, открытия. Потом поменяйтесь и найдите ошибки друг друга.

## Запомни
- Пассив = be в нужном времени + V3: *is made, was being made, has been made, will be made.*
- Continuous-пассив не пропускай: *is being repaired* (✅), *is repairing* (❌).
- С модальными: *must be done* (сейчас), *should have been done* (в прошлом).
- Глаголы без дополнения (happen, arrive, die, sleep) и глаголы состояния (have, fit, suit) пассива не имеют: ❌ *it was happened*.
- Have / get something done — услуга или неприятность: *I had my hair cut.* Без have получается, что ты сделала это сама.
- Новостной стиль: *It is said that he is rich. = He is said to be rich.* Для более раннего события: *He is said to have left.*
- Русское безличное «мне сказали, меня попросили, здесь говорят» передаётся пассивом: *I was told, I was asked, English is spoken here.*
- Born — всегда пассив; о рождении человека — Past Simple: *I was born in 1995.*
